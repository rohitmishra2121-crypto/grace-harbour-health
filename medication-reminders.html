Care Checklist

4 Signs Your Loved One Is Struggling With Their Medications

Spotting these early red flags can prevent severe health complications and avoidable emergency room visits.

1

Unopened Blister Slots

Finding past dates untouched in weekly dosette boxes or blister packs, indicating missed morning or evening doses.

2

Expired or Duplicate Bottles

Medicine cabinets cluttered with expired prescriptions, duplicate pills, or mixed bottles causing daily confusion.

3

Unexplained Dizziness & Falls

Sudden balance issues, brain fog, or low blood pressure often trace back to improper dosage timing or missed heart medication.

4

Difficulty Opening Containers

Physical barriers like arthritis, tremors, or poor eyesight making opening push-and-turn caps painful or impossible.

Our Commitment to Safe In-Home Care Standards

In Nova Scotia, Grace Harbour caregivers provide supportive non-medical reminders, packaging opening, hydration assistance, and daily logging.
