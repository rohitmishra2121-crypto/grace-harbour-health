Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

Clear, honest answers to help your family make informed in-home care decisions.

What is the difference between Personal Care and Companionship?

Companionship covers non-hands-on support such as social visits, tea time, mental stimulation, board games, walks, and accompaniment to community outings. Personal Care involves hands-on assistance with intimate activities of daily living (ADLs), such as bathing, showering, dressing, grooming, hygiene, and transfer support.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

Never. We understand that health conditions, family schedules, and recovery progress change constantly. Grace Harbour provides care on flexible terms with zero long-term commitments. You can adjust, increase, or pause your hours anytime.

How are Grace Harbour caregivers vetted and trained?

Every caregiver undergoes an exhaustive 5-step screening process: certified criminal background checks, vulnerable sector screening, reference verification, credential audits, and structured in-person interviews assessing empathy and communication skills. All staff are bonded and insured.

How much do in-home senior care services cost in Nova Scotia?

Our rates range between $32 and $42 per hour depending on the intensity of support required (such as general companionship vs. specialized two-person transfer personal care) and visit frequency. We provide clear, itemized invoicing with no hidden placement fees.

Can in-home care be covered by private health insurance or benefits?

Many private extended health insurance policies, Veterans Affairs Canada (VAC) benefits, and long-term care insurance policies cover home health and personal care services. We provide detailed receipts and care documentation to assist your claims.

What happens if our regular caregiver is sick or unavailable?

We prioritize consistency and assign primary caregivers to build strong bonds. However, if your regular caregiver is unwell or taking scheduled leave, our local care coordination team provides a fully briefed backup caregiver so your loved one's care routine remains uninterrupted.
