Frequently Asked Questions About Our Care

Everything you need to know about setting up care with Grace Harbour.

Can we combine multiple services into one care visit?

Yes, absolutely. In fact, most families do. A typical 3-hour morning visit often begins with bathing and dressing assistance, followed by preparing a wholesome breakfast, and finishes with a shared cup of tea or light laundry. Your hourly rate stays the same.

Are there contracts or minimum hour commitments?

We have zero long-term contracts. Visits typically have a 2 to 3 hour minimum to ensure our caregivers can deliver unhurried, meaningful care, but you can scale your schedule up, down, or pause care anytime without financial penalties.

How are your caregivers vetted and bonded?

Every employee undergoes certified criminal background and vulnerable sector checks, character reference verifications, and CPR/First Aid certification audits. All personnel are covered under comprehensive commercial general liability and workers' compensation insurance.

What areas in Nova Scotia do you serve?

We actively provide in-home care across Halifax Regional Municipality (Halifax, Dartmouth, Bedford, Sackville, Cole Harbour, Clayton Park) and surrounding Nova Scotia communities. Call our care intake team at (604) 379-4810 for prompt local scheduling.
