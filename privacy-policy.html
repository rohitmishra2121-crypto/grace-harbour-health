Privacy Policy

Grace Harbour Home Health Inc. is deeply committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our clients, their family representatives, and our caregivers across Nova Scotia.

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

At Grace Harbour Home Health Inc. (“Grace Harbour”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), we recognize that in-home care requires an exceptional degree of trust. In order to provide personalized, non-medical home care and daily living assistance to clients across Nova Scotia, we must collect, use, and maintain certain personal and health-related details.

We hold ourselves to the highest ethical and legal standards of confidentiality. We will never sell, lease, or commercially exploit any personal or health information entrusted to us.

2. Scope & Applicable Laws

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal information collected by Grace Harbour Home Health through our website (graceharbourhealth.ca), telephone consultations, in-home care assessments, and ongoing service delivery.

Our privacy practices are governed by Canadian federal and provincial legislation, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and align with the principles established under Nova Scotia’s Personal Health Information Act (PHIA).

3. Personal Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to assess, deliver, and coordinate safe, individualized non-medical care:

  • Contact & Identifying Information: Full name, physical residential address, telephone numbers, and email address of both the client and designated family representatives.
  • Care Assessment Details: Daily living routines, physical mobility status, assistance required (bathing, dressing, transfer assistance), fall risk considerations, dietary preferences, and cognitive comfort notes.
  • Emergency Contacts: Names and phone numbers of primary family caregivers, substitute decision-makers, and attending primary care physicians.
  • Administrative & Billing Data: Payment methods, service shift logs, invoicing records, and insurance provider claim details where applicable.
  • Job Applicant Data: Resumes, employment histories, CPR/First Aid certificates, references, and Vulnerable Sector Police Records Checks submitted by employment candidates.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for transparent caregiving purposes:

  • Developing and updating your custom non-medical care plan
  • Carefully matching vetted caregivers based on personality compatibility, language, and specific care skills
  • Delivering safe, punctual daily assistance and emergency escalation if a client becomes unwell
  • Providing shift wellness summaries to designated family members and authorized guardians
  • Processing client payments and issuing invoices
  • Complying with provincial healthcare regulations and safety auditing standards

5. Consent & Your Choices

We collect personal information with your knowledge and consent, or the consent of an authorized substitute decision-maker (such as a Power of Attorney for Personal Care or Legal Guardian).

Consent may be expressed in writing, verbally during phone intake, or implied when you voluntarily submit an inquiry on our website. You have the right to withdraw or modify your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice.

6. Disclosure & Sharing Limits

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Disclosure is strictly limited to:

  • Assigned Care Workers: Our bonded caregivers receive only the information necessary to provide safe, dignified assistance during their assigned shifts.
  • Designated Family Contacts: We share progress notes and updates only with individuals explicitly authorized by the client or legal representative.
  • Emergency Personnel: In the event of a medical emergency or immediate threat to life and safety, relevant information will be shared with emergency first responders (paramedics, hospitals).
  • Legal Requirements: Where required by valid subpoena, court order, or applicable Canadian federal or provincial law.

7. Security & Safeguards

We protect your personal information against loss, theft, unauthorized access, copying, use, or modification through multi-layered security controls:

Administrative

Strict staff confidentiality agreements, role-based access restrictions, and privacy training.

Technical

SSL/TLS encryption for web traffic, firewalls, and secure Canadian cloud data hosting.

Physical

Locked physical storage, clean desk policies, and restricted facility access.

8. Retention & Secure Disposal

We retain personal and care records only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which they were collected, or as mandated by Nova Scotia legal and financial record-keeping requirements. Once records are no longer required, they are securely shredded or digitally erased using industry-standard sanitization methods.

9. Individual Access & Correction Rights

Under Canadian privacy legislation, you have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and to ask for corrections if you believe any details are inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date.

To make an access or correction request, please submit a written inquiry to our Privacy Officer at info@graceharbourhealth.ca. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

10. Website Usage, Analytics & Cookies

When you visit our website, standard web server logs and privacy-preserving analytics may collect non-identifiable technical data (such as browser type, operating system, and pages viewed). We use this aggregate data solely to improve navigation, mobile responsiveness, and accessibility for families searching for home care. You can manage or disable cookies at any time via your browser settings.
