Careers at
Grace Harbour Home Health

At Grace Harbour Home Health, we believe great care starts with great people. We are building a team of compassionate, reliable, and respectful professionals who are committed to helping individuals live safely and with dignity in their own homes across Nova Scotia.

If you value empathy, professionalism, and meaningful work that genuinely changes lives, we’d love to hear from you. We offer flexible scheduling, respectful working environments, and a clear scope of care designed for staff well-being.

Why Work With Grace Harbour Home Health

We prioritize our caregivers and support workers because quality care is impossible without empowered, respected staff.

Respectful, Inclusive Workplace

A diverse and welcoming team where every voice is heard, personal backgrounds are celebrated, and staff dignity is safeguarded.

Flexible Scheduling

Days, evenings, weekends, and overnight awake shifts. We work around your personal commitments, family life, or studies.

Clear Scope of Care & Boundaries

Transparent expectations. No unreasonable shift demands or clinical procedures outside non-medical home support boundaries.

Make a tangible difference in the everyday lives of seniors, vulnerable adults, and grateful families in your local community.

Commitment to Staff Safety

Prior safety assessments in every home before assignment, 24/7 care coordinator on-call support, and emergency protocols.

Competitive Compensation & Growth

Competitive hourly rates, paid orientation, and continuing development opportunities in home care coordination.

Personal Care Full-time / Part-time / Casual Nova Scotia (HRM & Surrounding)

Personal Care Worker (PCW) / Continuing Care Assistant (CCA)

Provide non-medical personal care and daily living support to clients in their homes, promoting comfort, safety, and dignity.

Key Responsibilities

  • • Assist with bathing, grooming, dressing, and toileting
  • • Support mobility, transfers, and safe positioning
  • • Provide companionship and emotional support
  • • Assist with meal preparation and light housekeeping
  • • Offer medication reminders (non-administration)
  • • Observe and report changes in client condition
  • • Respect client dignity, privacy, and cultural preferences

Qualifications & Requirements

  • • PCW or CCA certificate (preferred) or equivalent experience
  • • Current First Aid & CPR certification
  • • Clean Vulnerable Sector Check / Criminal Record Check
  • • Reliable transportation (preferred)
  • • Strong communication and compassionate interpersonal skills
Companionship Part-time / Casual Nova Scotia

Companion / Home Support Worker

Focus on companionship, friendly conversation, and non-personal care support. Ideal for individuals with strong people skills, retirees, or students in health and social fields.

Key Responsibilities

  • • Provide rich companionship, lively conversation, and mental engagement
  • • Accompany clients to appointments, grocery shopping, or community walks
  • • Assist with light housekeeping, tidying, and errands
  • • Support simple meal preparation and kitchen tidying
  • • Provide primary caregiver relief and peaceful respite

Qualifications

  • • Experience working with seniors or vulnerable individuals (an asset)
  • • Compassionate, patient, and warm-hearted nature
  • • Strong communication skills and active listening
  • • Background check required
Overnight Care Part-time / Casual / Awake Night Shifts Nova Scotia

Overnight Support Worker

Provide non-medical nighttime supervision and assistance, ensuring comfort, safety, fall prevention, and calm reassurance during evening and overnight hours.

Key Responsibilities

  • • Provide watchful presence, companionship, and reassurance overnight
  • • Assist with nighttime toileting, transfers, and hydration
  • • Monitor comfort, prevent wander risk, and ensure bedroom safety
  • • Communicate morning updates to families and care coordinators

Qualifications

  • • Experience in home care or personal support preferred
  • • Current First Aid & CPR certification
  • • Proven ability to remain attentive on overnight shifts
  • • Calm, attentive, dependable, and punctual
Coordination & Admin Part-time / Casual / Hybrid Remote / Hybrid (Nova Scotia Based Preferred)

Care Coordinator / Intake Specialist

Serve as the vital bridge between clients, families, and care staff, ensuring smooth intake, careful caregiver matching, and seamless service delivery.

Key Responsibilities

  • • Conduct telephone intake consultations and initial client assessments
  • • Develop, maintain, and adjust personalized non-medical care plans
  • • Coordinate staff scheduling, coverage, and caregiver-client matching
  • • Communicate regularly with family members and referral partners
  • • Maintain accurate, confidential documentation and client files

Qualifications

  • • Background in health administration, social work, or community care
  • • Outstanding organizational, time-management, and phone skills
  • • Experience in home care or community health services an asset
  • • High comfort with scheduling software, databases, and secure records
Community Partnerships Part-time / Casual / Hybrid Nova Scotia Based

Community Outreach & Partnerships Coordinator

Build awareness and enduring relationships with local hospitals, senior centres, immigrant integration programs, and healthcare referral sources across Nova Scotia.

Key Responsibilities

  • • Cultivate relationships with hospital discharge teams & community groups
  • • Streamline intake referral pathways for seniors transitioning home
  • • Represent Grace Harbour Home Health at local healthcare events
  • • Coordinate educational materials, presentations, and partnership outreach

Qualifications

  • • Experience in community engagement, public relations, or partnerships
  • • Exceptional communication, networking, and presentation abilities
  • • In-depth knowledge of Nova Scotia's healthcare and community landscape
Advisory & Quality Contract / As Needed Non-Operational Consultant

Clinical Advisor (Consultant – Non-Operational Role)

Provide professional guidance and advisory oversight to ensure safe, ethical, and fully compliant non-medical care practices and protocols.

Key Responsibilities

  • • Review internal non-medical care protocols, policies, and health standards
  • • Provide training guidance for personal support workers (non-clinical scope)
  • • Advise on incident escalation, emergency procedures, and fall safety

Qualifications

  • • Licensed healthcare professional in good standing (RN, LPN, or equivalent)
  • • Extensive experience in community health, geriatric, or home care
  • • Thorough understanding of non-medical vs. clinical scope-of-practice boundaries
