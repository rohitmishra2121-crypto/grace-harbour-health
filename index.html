Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about home care options, pricing, or caregiver selection? Here are straightforward answers.

How quickly can care services begin?

In most circumstances, we can conduct an in-home assessment and place a dedicated caregiver within 24 to 48 hours. For hospital discharge recovery and urgent situations, same-day coordination can often be arranged.

Are your caregivers bonded, insured, and background checked?

Yes, 100%. Every caregiver employed by Grace Harbour undergoes strict criminal record and vulnerable sector checks, credential verification, character reference audits, and is covered by comprehensive commercial general liability insurance.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. We believe care should always adapt to your family's needs. We do not lock you into rigid long-term contracts. You can increase, decrease, pause, or adjust hours as your loved one’s health and lifestyle evolve.

How do you match a caregiver with my loved one?

During our Free In-Home Assessment, we assess not just physical care needs, but personality, life history, hobbies, and communication style. We then pair your loved one with a caregiver who fits their personality and interests, ensuring genuine warmth and companionship.

What regions in Nova Scotia do you serve?

We provide care services across Halifax Regional Municipality (Halifax, Dartmouth, Bedford, Sackville) and surrounding Nova Scotia communities. Please contact our care intake line at (604) 379-4810 to confirm availability in your specific area.
