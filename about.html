About Grace Harbour Home Health

Compassionate Personal Care.
Dignity at Home.

“Where care meets compassion—and home remains home.”

Grace Harbour Home Health provides trusted, non-medical personal care and daily living support services designed to help individuals live safely, comfortably, and with dignity in their own homes across Nova Scotia.

We support seniors, families, newcomers, and individuals recovering from illness or surgery by delivering compassionate, culturally responsive care—right where it matters most: at home. We believe aging or healing should never mean giving up independence, privacy, or familiar routines.

How We Work Every Day

Our Core Values: Built on Dignity & Compassion

We know inviting someone into your home is an act of deep trust. Every member of the Grace Harbour team adheres to a strict standard of ethics, empathy, and professional integrity.

Dignity First

We respect personal boundaries, modesty, and preferences. We encourage self-reliance wherever possible and provide gentle, unhurried help when needed.

Bonded & Screened Reliability

Every care worker undergoes comprehensive reference checks, vulnerable sector background screening, and CPR/First Aid verification.

Culturally Responsive Care

Nova Scotia is rich in diversity. We pride ourselves on honoring cultural traditions, dietary restrictions, and communication preferences.

Transparent & Open Communication

Families receive regular shift updates and wellness notes. No surprises, no hidden fees, and zero long-term contract lock-in.

Why Grace Harbour

The Grace Harbour Standard of Care

See how our dedicated, locally managed home care model protects your loved one's wellness and your family's peace of mind.

Personalized Care Assessments

We begin with a thorough in-home consultation to understand personal routines, mobility limitations, dietary preferences, and family expectations.

Consistent Caregiver Matching

We avoid revolving-door staff. We match clients with regular, dedicated caregivers based on personality compatibility, language, and specific care skills.

Flexible Hourly Coverage

From a 3-hour morning routine visit to 12-hour overnight supervision or 24/7 care, our plans adapt dynamically as your family's circumstances evolve.
