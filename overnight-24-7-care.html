Why Nighttime Care Matters

The Danger of Unassisted Nights for Nova Scotia Seniors

Dark hallways, sleep medication grogginess, and urinary urgency create the perfect storm for dangerous nighttime falls.

68%
Falls Happen Overnight

Over two-thirds of senior falls in Canadian homes occur between 9 PM and 6 AM, most commonly during hurried, unassisted trips to the bathroom.

75%+
Caregivers Suffer Sleep Deprivation

Adult children caring for aging parents lose an average of 3 hours of sleep per night, leading to chronic caregiver fatigue and burnout.

#1 Reason
For Premature Facility Placement

Night wandering, sundowning confusion, and fear of parents falling in the dark are the primary catalyst forcing families into long-term care homes.

Self-Assessment

4 Red Flags That Indicate It's Time for Overnight Care

If you or your loved one are experiencing these symptoms, overnight support can prevent a devastating emergency.

1

Frequent Night Bathroom Trips

Waking 3 or more times per night with unsteady legs, dizziness, or grogginess on dark hallway floors.

2

Sundowning & Exit-Seeking

Heightened evening confusion, packing bags to "go home," wandering out of bed, or leaving doors unlocked.

3

Midnight Kitchen Incidents

Leaving the stove burner on, mistaking daytime for nighttime, or rummaging through cupboards at 2 AM.

4

Caregiver Exhaustion

Spouse or adult child collapsing from broken sleep, unable to function safely at work or care for their own health.
